How a session runs

  1. 01

    Register with your university details

    Name, year of study and a username. Your password is stored hashed and kept apart from the rest of your record.

  2. 02

    Choose a theme, then a challenge

    Each theme holds six challenges — networking, forensics, web — and each one is harder than the last.

  3. 03

    Work the challenge while the clock runs

    Opening a challenge clones a VM on Proxmox and starts your timer. Submit flags as you find them; points land on the leaderboard straight away.

  4. 04

    Close it out

    Capture every flag and it is recorded as complete. Leave early and it is recorded as abandoned, with the time you spent either way.
